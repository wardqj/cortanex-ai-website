Technical depth.
A practical point of view.

Built in Jordan. Built around your operation.

We are a technology company designing and implementing operational software and AI-enabled systems.

We bring workflow design, software engineering and implementation together, combining previous client delivery experience with the development of ManuQube.

Can we begin with one workflow?

Yes. An engagement can focus on a specific operational problem, with agreed scope and acceptance criteria for the first implementation.

Can you work with our existing systems?

We assess your software, data and available interfaces. Integration requirements, access and limitations are established within the agreed scope.

Where does AI fit?

We start with a defined task and the information it needs. Its role, review points and boundaries are agreed for the implementation. AI assistance can support analysis and access to operational information.

What is ManuQube’s current stage?

MES, OMS, RMS and QMS have initial software releases. Tested handoffs, deployment hardening and factory pilot validation are the next stage. Warehouse and maintenance management form part of the wider roadmap.
